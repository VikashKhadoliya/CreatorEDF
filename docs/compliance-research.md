## 2. Service-Export Declaration Framework: Regulation 3(2) Analysis

Regulation 3(2) of FEMA 23(R)/2026-RB governs the declaration obligations for the export of services:

### 2.1 Resident Individuals: Regulation 3(2)(a)
- **Statutory Provision**: Under Regulation 3(2)(a), an exporter of services who is a **resident individual** (defined in Regulation 2(1)(da) as *"a person resident in India who is a natural person"*) is **deemed to have submitted the Export Declaration Form (EDF)** when they provide the required payment-receipt information to their Authorised Dealer bank for crediting their account.
- **Critical Legal Boundary**: **There is no monetary limit or ₹10 Lakh cap for resident individuals.** Resident individual creators, freelancers, and independent digital publishers receive deemed-submission treatment regardless of whether the remittance is ₹50,000 or ₹50,00,000.
- **Workflow in CreatorEDF**: The software must not state that an EDF is legally compulsory for resident individuals. Instead, it provides conditional guidance: the EDF Annexure is generated for the creator's records or if their AD bank explicitly requires an executed declaration to release funds or issue an e-FIRS under internal risk protocols.

### 2.2 Non-Individual Exporters: Regulation 3(2)(b)
- **Statutory Provision**: Under Regulation 3(2)(b), an exporter of services other than a resident individual (e.g. LLPs, Private Limited Companies, Partnership Firms) is **deemed to have submitted the EDF** when:
  $$\text{Inward Remittance Value for that Service} \le ₹10\text{ Lakh (or its foreign currency equivalent)}$$
  and the exporter provides the required payment-receipt information to their Authorised Dealer bank for crediting their account.
- **Transactions Exceeding ₹10 Lakh**: Where the inward remittance exceeds ₹10 Lakh, the deemed-submission concession under Regulation 3(2)(b) does not apply. In such cases, the **normal statutory EDF obligation under Regulation 3(2)** applies.
- **Deferral to AD Bank Operating Guidelines**: While Regulation 3(2) establishes the baseline obligation to furnish export declarations, the exact operational procedure (such as whether the bank requires an electronic portal submission, physical signed Annexure, or specific trade desk documentation) is governed by the Authorised Dealer Category-I bank's internal trade processing instructions. CreatorEDF prepares the standardized Form EDF (Section 2B) to satisfy this baseline obligation.

### 2.3 Single Consolidated Monthly EDF: Regulation 3(2)(c)
- **Statutory Provision**: **Regulation 3(2)(c) explicitly provides that an exporter of services who exports services to one or more recipients in a month may submit a single EDF covering all such exports.**
- **Submission Timeline**: The single monthly declaration must be submitted within **30 days from the end of the month** in which the invoices for the services were raised.
- **Extension Authority**: The AD bank may, upon request by the exporter showing reasonable cause, grant an extension for submitting the EDF.
- **Legal Correction**: **Separate EDFs are NOT legally compulsory for every distinct overseas recipient.** An Indian creator receiving payments from YouTube/Google, Meta, and Patreon in the same calendar month may lawfully group these transactions into a single monthly EDF under Regulation 3(2)(c), listing each recipient and invoice in Section 2B. Commercial tax invoices, however, remain individual per contracting entity for commercial and GST accounting purposes.

## 3. Specified Authorities: Regulation 2(1)(f) Verification

Under Regulation 2(1)(f) of FEMA 23(R)/2026-RB, the "specified authority" empowered to certify or process export declarations is strictly segregated by subject matter and economic zone:

| Export Category | Specified Authority in Domestic Tariff Area (DTA) | Specified Authority in Special Economic Zone (SEZ) | Legal Implications for CreatorEDF |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Goods** | Commissioner of Customs | Development Commissioner of SEZ | Non-applicable to digital service creators; customs ports marked `"Not applicable"`. |
| **Services (other than software)** | **An Authorised Dealer (AD Bank)** | Development Commissioner of SEZ | AD Category-I banks are the primary specified authority for digital creators, YouTubers, advertisers, and digital publishers. |
| **Software** | **An Authorised Dealer OR Software Technology Parks of India (STPI)** | Development Commissioner of SEZ | **STPI and AD Banks remain designated authorities.** CreatorEDF does not claim that SOFTEX is universally eliminated; software exports continue to follow statutory instructions administered through STPI / designated MeitY channels or AD banks as applicable. |

## 4. Realisation and Settlement Timelines: Regulation 5(1)

Under Regulation 5(1) of FEMA 23(R)/2026-RB (as amended by Notification No. FEMA 23(R)/(1)/2026-RB):

1. **Normal Realisation Period — Nine (9) Months**:
   - The full value of goods, software, or services exported shall be realised and repatriated to India within **nine (9) months** from the date of export (invoice date).
   - *Statutory Rule*: Applies to all standard foreign currency remittances (USD, EUR, GBP, SGD, etc.).
   - *Formula*:
     $$\text{Statutory Deadline} = \text{Invoice Date} + 9\text{ Calendar Months}$$
2. **Indian Rupee (INR) Settlement Period — Twelve (12) Months**:
   - Where export transactions are invoiced and settled in Indian Rupees through the **Special Rupee Vostro Account (SRVA)** mechanism per RBI Circular No. 10 (July 11, 2022), the prescribed realization period is **twelve (12) months** from the date of export.

## 5. GST Architecture: Rule 46 vs Rule 96A & SAC Code Selection

### 5.1 Rule 46 (Statutory Tax Invoice Requirements)
Mandatory for all GST-registered creators. Enforces:
- Consecutive serial number ($\le 16$ alphanumeric characters, unique per FY).
- Date of issue.
- Supplier Legal Name, Registered Address, and GSTIN.
- Recipient Legal Name, Address, Delivery Address, and Destination Country.
- Applicable Services Accounting Code (SAC).
- Total invoice value and taxable value in transaction currency.

### 5.2 Rule 96A (Letter of Undertaking / LUT Endorsement — Strictly Conditional)
Under CGST Rule 96A(1), the statutory endorsement:
> **"SUPPLY MEANT FOR EXPORT UNDER BOND OR LETTER OF UNDERTAKING WITHOUT PAYMENT OF INTEGRATED TAX"**

**must only appear when legally applicable**. It must **never** be applied as a universal default:
1. **GST Registered with Active LUT (Form GST RFD-11)**:
   - Mandatory endorsement included on invoice, along with the exporter's LUT ARN (Application Reference Number) and relevant Financial Year.
2. **GST Registered Exporting on Payment of Integrated Tax (IGST)**:
   - Endorsement states: `"SUPPLY MEANT FOR EXPORT ON PAYMENT OF INTEGRATED TAX"`.
3. **Unregistered Creator (Turnover Below ₹20L / ₹10L Exemption Limit)**:
   - Exporter issues a **Commercial Export Invoice**.
   - **No LUT endorsement is printed**, preventing unlawful claims under CGST Rule 96A. GSTIN is marked `"URP"` (Unregistered Person) or `"Not Applicable"`.

### 5.3 Configurable SAC Selection (No Universal Default)
Digital creators engage in varied monetization activities. **SAC `998361` must not be hardcoded or universally assigned.** CreatorEDF provides a configurable SAC selector requiring explicit user confirmation:

| SAC Code | Description | Creator Monetization Activity |
|---|---|---|
| **`998361`** | Advertising services | Standard YouTube AdSense display ads, video overlay ads, brand integrations. |
| **`998365`** | Internet advertising space and timeshare | Website banner advertising, direct sponsorship display slots. |
| **`998439`** | Other online content and database access supply | Paid newsletters, digital guides, digital assets, online courses. |
| **`999611`** | Sound recording and music licensing | Music streaming payouts, royalty earnings, podcast syndication. |
| **`999612`** | Motion picture, video production services | Commissioned video production, documentary editing, brand production. |
| **`998311`–`998319`** | Information Technology / Software development | SaaS platforms, custom software code, plugin development. |
